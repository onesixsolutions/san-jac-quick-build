## Typography

### Official Typefaces
- **Sabon** — wordmark font (Bold for wordmark, Italic for tagline)
- **Gotham** — primary marketing sans-serif
- **Frutiger** — secondary sans-serif
- **Montserrat** — web/digital alternative

### Acceptable Alternatives
- Garamond (serif alternative)
- Arial (sans-serif alternative)
- Helvetica
- Times New Roman

---

## Logo

- Two approved versions: with tagline and without tagline
- Wordmark font: **Sabon Bold**; tagline font: **Sabon Italic**
- Service mark (SM) is required in upper-right corner
- Primary color: San Jac Blue (Pantone 2945)
- Minimum print size: **1.5 inches wide**
- Clear zone: measured by height of the "C" in "College"
- The interlocking SJC symbol may be used alone only in extreme space constraints

### Logo Don'ts
- Do not alter logo colors
- Do not stretch or disproportionately scale
- Do not recreate in another typeface
- Do not link with another logo without marketing guidance
- Do not use a different tagline

---

## Mascot — The Raven

- Adopted college-wide in 2023
- Two logo versions: Raven Head and Raven in Flight
- Always use the raven illustration together with the "San Jac Ravens" wordmark — never standalone
- Colors: San Jac Blue (PMS 2945), San Jac Gold (PMS 123), Process Black, Process White
- Minimum logo size: **1.25 inches wide**
- Do not use AI-generated Raven artwork

## Writing Guidelines

### Do
- Lead with student outcomes and mission alignment
- Use bite-sized content and bullet points — audiences scan
- Spell out acronyms on first reference, then use acronym alone
- Use relatable stories and compelling visuals

### Don't
- Use "SJC" as an abbreviation
- Write long run-on sentences
- Use acronyms without defining them first
- Use "San Jac College" or "San Jacinto" alone

### Grammar & Style Notes
- **advisor** — always with "-or" ending (not "adviser")
- **College** — capitalize on second reference when referring to San Jacinto College
- **academic degrees** — use phrases over abbreviations: "a doctorate in psychology" vs. "Ph.D."
- **associate degree** — no possessive; bachelor's degree / master's degree use apostrophe
- **Campus names** — always capitalize: North Campus, Central Campus, South Campus, Generation Park Campus, Maritime Campus, San Jac Online

---

## Photography

- Choose images that create emotional connection
- Incorporate San Jac Blue and Gold into image compositions when possible
- Use natural lighting; incorporate bokeh and dynamic angles
- Include diverse representation of students, faculty, and staff
- Include a mix of medium, detail, and close-up shots
- No busy backgrounds behind logos
- Program photos must show proper PPE
