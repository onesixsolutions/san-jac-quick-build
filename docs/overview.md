# San Jac Snowflake Demo — Overview

## Objective
Demonstrate Snowflake Cortex AI capabilities applied to Higher Education workflows at San Jacinto College.

## Key Capabilities to Showcase
1. **Cortex Search** — semantic search over unstructured documents (policy docs, handbooks, catalogs)
2. **Cortex Analyst** — natural language queries over structured enrollment/analytics data
3. **Streamlit in Snowflake** — zero-infrastructure AI app deployment
4. **Snowpark / Python** — custom ML and data pipelines natively in Snowflake

## Audience
- IT leadership
- Academic affairs / advising leadership
- Data & analytics team

## Demo Flow (Suggested)
1. Ingest sample Higher Ed documents into a Snowflake stage
2. Stand up Cortex Search service — show semantic Q&A
3. Connect Cortex Analyst to enrollment/course data — show NL-to-SQL
4. Launch Streamlit app as the unified front-end
