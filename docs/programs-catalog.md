## Non-Credit / Continuing & Professional Development (CPD)

CPD serves workforce training and lifelong learning. As of 2026, CPD is organized into four centers:

| Center | Focus |
|--------|-------|
| **EDGE Center** | Aerospace, aviation, and advanced manufacturing |
| **BIT Center** | IT, AI, business, entrepreneurship, and logistics |
| **Corporate Center** | Apprenticeships, grants, and cross-sector training |
| **Community & Health Center** | Adult education, healthcare, youth, and senior programs |

### Program Areas

**Aerospace & Aviation (EDGE Center)**
Official education training partner for the Houston Spaceport at Ellington Airport. Offerings include FAA Part 107 Drone Pilot certification, composite manufacturing, aerospace quality technician training, and aircraft structure technology.

**Biotechnology / Biomanufacturing**
Sole NIBRT-licensed training provider in the southern United States. Prepares professionals for the biomanufacturing workforce.

**Center for Petrochemical, Energy & Technology**
Serves the regional energy and industrial workforce — a major CPD program area for the Houston area.

**Maritime**
50+ courses approved by the U.S. Coast Guard (USCG), following STCW international standards. Covers inland towboats to large commercial vessels.

**Applied Trades**
Welding, truck driving, pipefitting, HVAC — industry-recognized standards.

**Computers / IT Training**
Dedicated non-credit IT program area through CPD.

**Health & Safety Occupations**
Non-credit health and safety professional training.

**Education / ESL & English Programs**
English Communication Skills: ESL Beginning A through ESL Advanced B. Conversational English. Serves students whose primary language is not English.

**Business & Professions**
Non-credit business and professional development open enrollment courses.

**Lifelong Learning**
Personal development and enrichment: dance, martial arts, retirement planning, motorcycle riding, gardening, ukulele, and more.

**Online CPD Courses**
Available via the Ed2Go portal — register through CPD, access at ed2go.com.

**Summer Camps (Youth)**
STEM, athletics, music, and career exploration camps for youth.

## CPD Enrollment Process

1. Must be 18+ years old; no formal college application required for most non-credit courses
2. Register online, in person, by phone (281-542-2020), or by email (registration.cpd@sjcd.edu)
3. Payment due same day as registration; credit/debit cards accepted
4. Some programs require background check (~$60) and/or drug screen (~$50)
5. Financial aid: Texas Public Education Grant (TPEG) and Workforce Solutions scholarships available

**"Linked" courses:** CPD courses held alongside credit courses — completers receive academic credit if they later enroll in a degree/certificate program.
