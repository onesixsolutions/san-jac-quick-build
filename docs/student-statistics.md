## Demographics

### Gender
- Female: 59% (18,786 students)
- Male: 41% (~13,026 students)
- Consistent 59/41 split across all five years reviewed

### Race & Ethnicity
| Group | % |
|-------|---|
| Hispanic or Latino | 63.3–63.4% (~20,158 students) |
| White | 15.2% (declining: 6,235 in 2020–21 → 4,831 in 2024–25) |
| Black or African American | 8.48% |
| Asian | 4.99% |
| Two or more races | 1.86% |
| American Indian/Alaska Native | <1% |
| Native Hawaiian/Pacific Islander | <1% |

> San Jacinto College is a designated **Hispanic-Serving Institution (HSI)**, making it eligible for federal grants supporting Hispanic Americans.

### Age Distribution
| Segment | Count |

| Under 25 | 25,315 |

- 45% of students are in the traditional college-age range (18–21), vs. 60% national average — indicating a notably non-traditional population

### Geography
- 97% of students reside in Texas
- Service area: East Harris County and surrounding communities

## Online Enrollment

| Category | Count |
|----------|-------|
| Exclusively online | 9,455 (30% of total) |
| Some online courses | ~10,134 |

**Trend:** Online-only enrollment spiked to 26,636 during COVID (2020–21), normalized to ~9,425 by 2022–23, and has held steady since. Online enrollment has grown 249% since 2015.

---

## Financial Profile

- **56%** of students receive Federal Pell Grant Aid (low-income designation)
- Texas Public Education Grant (TPEG) available for qualifying students
- Workforce Solutions scholarships available for high-growth occupations

---

## Graduation & Degrees

- Top Associate's Degree concentrations:
  1. General Studies — 1,253 degrees
  2. General Business — 679 degrees
  3. Behavioral Sciences — 350 degrees
- Highest graduation rate demographic: **female Asian students at 50%**
